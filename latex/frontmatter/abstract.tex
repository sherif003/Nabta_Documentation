\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
\markboth{Abstract}{Abstract}

\noindent
This project introduces \nabta, an intelligent, full-stack, production-ready, end-to-end artificial intelligence system designed for automated plant care and pathogen diagnosis. The proposed platform addresses a critical and globally pervasive challenge in modern agriculture: the timely and accurate identification of plant diseases under practical field conditions, a challenge responsible for the destruction of up to \textbf{40\%} of global crop yields on an annual basis.

The \nabta architecture integrates multiple computer vision models in a sequential pipeline. \gls{yolov8} performs real-time object detection to isolate leaf regions using high-confidence bounding boxes. A custom \gls{cnn} subsequently conducts fine-grained classification across \textbf{102} disease and healthy plant classes. Pixel-level segmentation is achieved using a \unet architecture with an EfficientNet-B0 encoder, producing refined tissue masks with a mean \gls{iou} of \textbf{91.8\%}. Removing background artefacts -- including soil, shadows, and unrelated vegetation -- before classification is a central contribution of the multi-stage pipeline design.

To address the interpretability gap prevalent in black-box deep learning systems, the platform incorporates \gls{gradcam} to generate gradient-weighted heatmaps that visually justify the model's classification decisions to non-technical end-users. Furthermore, a domain-restricted generative \gls{ai} chatbot, powered by the Cohere \gls{api} and \gls{rag}, provides expert-quality treatment prescriptions and agronomist-vetted consultations in both Arabic and English. All inference services are exposed through a high-performance, asynchronous \fastapi backend backed by a vector database, while the user-facing layer is delivered as a fully responsive React.js dashboard featuring historical analysis records, infection timeline visualisations, and a tiered subscription model.

Empirical evaluation demonstrates a \gls{cnn} classification accuracy of \textbf{96.4\%}, a \yolo mean Average Precision (\gls{map}) of \textbf{94.1\%}, and a \unet \gls{iou} of \textbf{91.8\%}, with end-to-end inference completing in under \textbf{0.4 seconds}. The \gls{llm} consultation module achieved a satisfaction score of \textbf{4.7 out of 5.0} as assessed by independent agricultural experts under blind evaluation conditions. These results collectively affirm the viability of \nabta as a deployable, scalable precision-agriculture solution capable of mitigating crop losses attributable to delayed or inaccurate disease diagnosis.

\vspace{1em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Plant disease detection, deep learning, YOLOv8, U-Net, convolutional neural networks, Grad-CAM, explainable AI, retrieval-augmented generation, FastAPI, smart agriculture, precision farming.
